# Florida 4-Point Insurance & Statutory Underwriting Guidelines
**Standard of 407flips (John)**

## 1. Roof Inspection & Binding Rules (F.S. § 627.7011 & SB 4-D)
* **Age Thresholds:**
  * Shingle roofs older than **15 years** receive automatic binding declination or mandatory replacement condition endorsements.
  * Tile and metal roofs older than **25 years** require certified inspection proving at least 3 to 5 years Remaining Useful Life (RUL).
* **Florida Building Code 25% Rule (SB 4-D Compliance):**
  * If more than 25% of any roof plane or section requires repair, Florida Building Code mandates a 100% full tear-off and replacement to current hurricane standards (including secondary water barrier underlayment and ring-shank nails).
* **Cost Deduction:** \$12,000 to \$16,000 capital outlay.

---

## 2. HVAC & Mechanical Mandates (DOE SEER2 & EPA AIM Act)
* **Florida Useful Life:** Average design life in Florida is only 10–12 years due to extreme humidity and thermal run hours.
* **Refrigerant Bans:**
  * R-22 Freon (pre-2010): Completely banned from production; servicing costs \$150–\$200/lb.
  * R-410A (2010–2024): Transitioned under EPA AIM Act effective Jan 1, 2025 to Low-GWP A2L refrigerants (R-454B). Mismatched component swaps (putting a new condenser on an old air handler) are non-compliant.
* **Efficiency Standards:** Minimum 14.3 SEER2 in Florida / Southeast region.
* **Cost Deduction:** \$8,000 to \$10,500 matched split-system replacement.

---

## 3. Plumbing Pipe Era Risks
* **Pre-1975 Builds (Cast Iron Drain Lines):**
  * Highly susceptible to bottom channeling, structural collapse, and hydrogen sulfide erosion from sewer gases.
  * Involuntary and voluntary carriers require a clean sewer camera scope video. Failure results in binding refusal or a total water-damage exclusion endorsement.
  * *Cost Deduction:* \$10,000 to \$18,000 (epoxy lining or slab trenching).
* **1978–1995 Builds (Polybutylene Supply Lines):**
  * Grey flexible "Quest" piping with plastic acetal or copper crimp fittings suffers micro-fractures from chlorinated tap water.
  * Citizens and private carriers refuse coverage or require a signed binding agreement to re-pipe within 30 days of closing.
  * *Cost Deduction:* \$5,000 to \$8,000 whole-house PEX repipe.

## 4. Electrical Panel & Wiring Hazards
* **Defective Panels (Automatic 4-Point Declination):**
  * **Federal Pacific Electric (FPE) Stab-Lok:** Breakers fail to trip under overload (proven fire hazard).
  * **Zinsco / Sylvania-Zinsco:** Aluminum bus bars overheat and melt to the breaker.
  * **Challenger Panels (1980s–early 1990s):** Overheating bussing at 15A/20A breakers.
  * **Fuse Boxes (60A / 100A):** Substandard service capacity for modern residential loads.
  * *Cost Deduction:* \$2,500 to \$4,000 200A service panel replacement.
* **Single-Strand Aluminum Branch Wiring (1965–1973):**
  * High risk of junction overheating. Requires AlumiConn 3-port lugs or Copalum crimping at all devices (\$2,500–\$4,500).

## 5. Retail Mortgage Disqualification (HUD Handbook 4000.1 / Fannie Mae B4-1)
* Missing kitchen cabinetry or appliances, non-functional electrical/plumbing utilities, interior drywall holes, open ceiling patches, rotten siding/soffits, or roof leaks fail Minimum Property Standards (MPS).
* **Market Reality:** Eliminates 100% of FHA, VA, and Conventional retail buyers. The property cannot close through mortgage financing and can only trade to all-cash investors.
