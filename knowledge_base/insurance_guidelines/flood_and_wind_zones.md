# Florida Flood Zones & Wind-Borne Debris Regions (Underwriting Guide)
**Standard of 407flips (John)**

In Florida, a property's flood designation and wind speed contour zone can add thousands of dollars in annual holding costs and tens of thousands in mandatory window/roof building code upgrades.

## 1. FEMA Flood Zone Designations & Insurance Mandates

| Flood Zone | Risk Level | Lender Mandate | Annual Carrying Cost Impact | Investor Underwriting Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zone X (Unshaded)** | Minimal Risk (Area of minimal flood hazard, above 500-yr flood level) | **NO mandatory flood insurance required** by federally regulated lenders. | **\$0 / Optional** (\$450–\$650/yr if buyer chooses voluntary coverage). | Ideal for retail flip exit and rental holding. |
| **Zone X (Shaded)** | Moderate Risk (500-year flood zone, or 100-yr with average depths < 1 ft) | **NO mandatory flood insurance required** by standard lenders. | **\$0 / Optional** (\$650–\$900/yr voluntary). | Safe for standard cash/conventional buyers. |
| **Zone AE (Special Flood Hazard Area - SFHA)** | **High Risk** (100-year base flood elevation defined) | **MANDATORY FLOOD INSURANCE** for all FHA, VA, and Conventional mortgages. | **+\$1,200 to \$3,800 / year** holding cost hit (NFIP Risk Rating 2.0). | **Must deduct flood premium** from buyer's cash-flow cap rate; retail buyers require Elevation Certificate (EC). |

| **Zone AH / AO** | High Risk Shallow (Ponding water 1–3 ft deep) | **MANDATORY FLOOD INSURANCE**. | **+\$1,500 to \$3,200 / year**. | Common in low-elevation central Florida retention areas. |

### Florida Underwriting Ammo on Flood Zone AE Deals:
1. **Lender Stoppage:** Conventional buyers cannot close without prepaid NFIP/Private flood insurance binder.
2. **Buyer Debt-to-Income (DTI) Shock:** An extra \$250/month flood premium lowers a retail buyer's purchasing power by ~\$35,000 in loan balance.

## 2. Florida Building Code (FBC) Wind-Borne Debris Regions (HVHZ & Wind Contours)

Florida Building Code enforces wind speed design maps (ASCE 7-22) that dictate roofing attachment, impact openings, and structural tie-downs.

### A. High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)
* **Counties:** **Miami-Dade and Broward Counties**.
* **Design Wind Speed:** **170 – 180+ mph**.
* **Impact Mandate:** 100% of all exterior window and door openings must have Miami-Dade NOA (Notice of Acceptance) certified impact glass or approved hurricane shutters.

### B. Wind-Borne Debris Region (Central & Coastal Florida)
* **Coastal Counties (Brevard, Pinellas, Hillsborough, Volusia, Palm Beach, Manatee, Sarasota):**
  * **Design Wind Speed:** **140 – 160 mph**.
  * **Mandate:** Any replacement window or glazed door within 1 mile of coastal mean high water or in $\ge$140 mph zones must be **Impact Rated** or protected with engineered shutters.
  * **Cost Impact:** Impact windows cost **\$850–\$1,300 per opening** vs. standard vinyl double-pane windows at **\$350–\$450 per opening**. Replacing 12 windows in a coastal block home adds a **\$12,000–\$15,000** capital outlay.
* **Inland Central Florida (Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Polk):**
  * **Design Wind Speed:** **130 – 140 mph**.
  * **Mandate:** Standard double-pane Low-E vinyl windows permitted outside the 1-mile wind-borne debris boundary. Roof nailing requires minimum 8d ring-shank nails spaced at 6" on center at edges.

## 3. Wind Mitigation Discount Matrix (The 7 Credits)
When pitching flippers or rental investors, scoring a **Florida Uniform Mitigation Verification Inspection (Form OIR-B1-1802)** cuts hazard insurance premiums by 30% to 55%:

1. **Roof Covering:** FBC compliant architectural shingles (2001 or newer code).
2. **Roof Deck Attachment:** Minimum 8d nails at 6"/6" spacing (Credit Level C).
3. **Roof to Wall Connection:** **Clips or Single/Double Wraps** (Worth \$800–\$1,500/yr in insurance savings vs. basic toenails).
4. **Roof Geometry:** **Hip Roof** (Slopes on all sides) receives up to a **40% automatic rate discount** compared to Gable roofs.
5. **Secondary Water Barrier (SWR):** Self-adhering polymer modified bitumen underlayment (peel-and-stick) across the entire decking.
6. **Opening Protection:** Class A impact ratings on all glazed openings (windows, sliding doors, garage door).
