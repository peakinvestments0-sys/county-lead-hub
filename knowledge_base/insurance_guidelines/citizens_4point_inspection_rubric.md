# Citizens Property Insurance 4-Point Inspection Rubric (Form M-4P)
**Standard of 407flips (John)**

This reference outlines the exact line-by-line failure criteria on the official **Citizens 4-Point Inspection Form (Insp4pt 01/23 / Form M-4P)** used across all Florida carriers. If an inspector marks "YES" to deficiencies in any of these sections, the binder is rejected until cured.

---

## SECTION 1: ELECTRICAL SYSTEM

### Required Information:
* Main Panel Capacity (Amps) & Voltage (100A, 150A, 200A)
* Main Panel Brand/Manufacturer
* Sub-panel Brand/Manufacturer
* Branch Wiring Material (Copper, Aluminum, Copper-Clad Aluminum)

### 🚨 Automatic 4-Point Declination Triggers:
1. **Unacceptable Panel Brands:**
   * **Federal Pacific Electric (FPE) Stab-Lok**
   * **Zinsco / Sylvania-Zinsco**
   * **Challenger** (Specifically panels with 15A/20A single-pole breakers prone to bus overheating)
   * **Fuse Boxes** (Open knife-blade or screw-in fuses with 60A/100A mains)
2. **Hazardous Branch Wiring:**
   * **Single-Strand Aluminum Branch Wiring (1965–1973):** Automatic decline unless 100% of branch terminations are remediated with **Copalum crimps** or **AlumiConn 3-port insulated lugs** (standard twist-on wire nuts are rejected).
   * **Cloth-Covered Romex (Unshielded/Aged):** Brittle insulation or frayed sheathing.
   * **Knob and Tube Wiring:** Automatic binder decline.
3. **Physical Panel Hazards:**
   * Double-tapped circuit breakers (two conductors in one terminal not rated for tandem use).
   * Scorching, burn marks, corrosion, or open breaker knockouts.

---

## SECTION 2: HEATING & AIR CONDITIONING (HVAC)

### Required Information:
* Primary Heating System Source (Central Heat Pump, Forced Air Electric, Gas, Wall Units)
* Age of System & Overall Condition (Satisfactory / Unsatisfactory)
* Date of Last Service / Update

### 🚨 Automatic 4-Point Declination Triggers:
1. **No Permanent Heating Source:** Portable space heaters or plug-in radiators do not qualify. Central heat pump or permanently installed electric strip heat is mandatory.
2. **Compressor / Air Handler Failure:** Unit non-operational, active refrigerant leak, heavy condensation overflow, or disconnected ductwork.
3. **Age & Refrigerant Obsolescence:**
   * Units exceeding 15 years with signs of mechanical rust/corrosion trigger mandatory inspection certification.
   * R-22 systems with cooling failure cannot be partially serviced under 2025/2026 EPA guidelines; matched split replacement is required.

---

## SECTION 3: PLUMBING SYSTEM

### Required Information:
* Supply Piping Material (Copper, PEX, CPVC, Galvanized, Polybutylene)
* Drain / Waste / Vent (DWV) Piping Material (PVC, ABS, Cast Iron, Galvanized)
* Water Heater Age, Capacity, and Temperature/Pressure Relief (TPR) Valve Discharge Pipe
* Presence of Active Leaks, Prior Leak Damage, or Water Staining

### 🚨 Automatic 4-Point Declination Triggers:
1. **Polybutylene Supply Lines ("Quest" Piping - 1978 to 1995):**
   * Grey flexible piping with plastic acetal or copper rings.
   * **Carrier Rule:** Citizens and private Florida carriers require whole-house repiping to PEX within 30 days of binding or refuse coverage outright.
2. **Cast Iron Drain Lines (Pre-1975 Builds):**
   * Heavy rust scale, flaking, bottom channeling, or persistent sewer backup history.
   * Major carriers mandate a clean 30-minute sewer camera video inspection before binding older homes.
3. **Galvanized Steel Supply Lines (Pre-1965):**
   * Corroded joints, rust discoloration in tap water, or low water pressure.
4. **Water Heater Issues:**
   * Water heater age > 15 years (many carriers mandate replacement at 12–15 yrs).
   * Missing or improperly routed TPR valve discharge line (must discharge within 6" of floor/ground).

---

## SECTION 4: ROOF INSPECTION

### Required Information:
* Primary Roof Covering Type (Architectural Shingles, 3-Tab, Concrete Tile, Metal, Flat/Built-Up)
* Age of Primary Roof & Secondary Roof Sections
* Documented Remaining Useful Life (RUL) in Years
* Any Evidence of Leaks, Missing Shingles, Damaged Flashing, or Granule Loss

### 🚨 Automatic 4-Point Declination Triggers:
1. **Shingle Roof Age > 15 Years (or Tile/Metal > 25 Years):**
   * Must have an inspector certified **$\ge$3 to 5 years Remaining Useful Life**. If inspector writes "<3 years" or "0 years", binding is rejected immediately.
2. **Visible Deficiencies:**
   * Curling shingles, severe granule erosion exposing fiberglass matting, moss/algae growth causing shingle rot.
   * Sagging roof decking, rotten fascia/soffit framing, ponding water on low-slope sections.
   * Missing flashing around vent pipes, chimneys, or valleys.
3. **Florida 25% Rule (SB 4-D):**
   * If damage exceeds 25% of the roof plane, piecemeal patching is prohibited; whole-roof tear-off is legally mandated by Florida Building Code.
