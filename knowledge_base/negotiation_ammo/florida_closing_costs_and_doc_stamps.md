# Florida Title Closing Costs & Doc Stamps Fee Schedule (2026)
**Standard of 407flips (John)**

This reference provides the exact statutory formulas and promulgated rates governing all real estate closing friction in Florida. Use these figures to calculate net sheets for sellers and cash closing sheets for buyers.

## 1. Documentary Stamp Taxes (Florida Statute § 201)

### A. Documentary Stamps on Deeds (Transfer Tax on Sale Price)
* **All Florida Counties (Except Miami-Dade):**
  * **Rate:** **\$0.70 per \$100.00** of gross purchase price (or fractional part thereof) $\rightarrow$ **0.70%**.
  * *Example on \$300,000 Purchase:* `($300,000 / 100) * $0.70 = $2,100.00`.
  * *Customary Payor:* Typically paid by the **Seller** on standard FAR/BAR contracts (negotiated to Buyer in wholesale/as-is assignments).
* **Miami-Dade County Exception:**
  * **Rate:** **\$0.60 per \$100.00** on single-family residences.
  * *Commercial / Other Properties:* \$0.60 per \$100 base + \$0.45 per \$100 surtax = **\$1.05 per \$100**.

### B. Documentary Stamps on Notes & Mortgages (Financing Friction)
* **Rate:** **\$0.35 per \$100.00** of promissory note value (rounded up to nearest \$100) $\rightarrow$ **0.35%**.
* *Example on \$200,000 Loan:* `($200,000 / 100) * $0.35 = $700.00`.
* *Payor:* Paid by the **Borrower / Buyer**.

### C. Florida Intangible Personal Property Tax (F.S. § 199.133)
* **Rate:** **2.0 mills** on each dollar of the just valuation of the mortgage or debt obligation $\rightarrow$ **0.002 (0.20%)**.
* *Example on \$200,000 Loan:* `$200,000 * 0.002 = $400.00`.
* *Payor:* Paid by the **Borrower / Buyer**.

## 2. Florida Promulgated Title Insurance Premium Rates (The Butler Rule)
Under Florida Administrative Code (F.A.C. 69O-186.003), title insurance premium rates are strictly regulated by the Florida Department of Financial Services (DFS):

| Purchase Price / Policy Bracket | Promulgated Title Rate | Cumulative Bracket Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First \$100,000** | **\$5.75 per \$1,000** | \$575.00 |
| **Over \$100,000 up to \$1,000,000** | **\$5.00 per \$1,000** | Up to \$5,075.00 |
| **Over \$1,000,000 up to \$5,000,000** | **\$2.50 per \$1,000** | Up to \$15,075.00 |
| **Over \$5,000,000 up to \$10,000,000** | **\$2.25 per \$1,000** | Up to \$26,325.00 |
| **Over \$10,000,000** | **\$2.00 per \$1,000** | — |

### Real Title Premium Calculation Examples:
* **\$200,000 Purchase:**  
  * First \$100k: `100 * $5.75 = $575`  
  * Next \$100k: `100 * $5.00 = $500`  
  * **Total Title Premium: \$1,075.00**
* **\$350,000 Purchase:**  
  * First \$100k: `100 * $5.75 = $575`  
  * Next \$250k: `250 * $5.00 = $1,250`  
  * **Total Title Premium: \$1,825.00**

## 3. Customary Settlement & Ancillary Closing Fees (Central Florida Market)
Title companies and real estate attorneys typically assess standard ancillary service fees:

* **Title Settlement / Closing Fee:** \$450 – \$650 (often split or paid by buyer in investor transactions).
* **Title Search & Municipal Lien Search:** \$225 – \$350 (uncovers municipal code violations, utility liens, unrecorded permits).
* **Florida County Recording Fees (Clerk of Court):**
  * First page: **\$10.00**
  * Each additional page: **\$8.50**
  * Standard 2-page Deed recording: **\$18.50**
  * Standard 14-page Mortgage recording: **\$120.50**
* **Electronic Recording / Courier / Wire Fees:** \$75 – \$125.
